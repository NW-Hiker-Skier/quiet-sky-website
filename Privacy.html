The plain-language version.

The long policy is below. The short version: we built Quiet Sky so there is nothing to sell, and we keep no record of your requests.

Last updated 3 June 2026

Hop 1 · You → our proxy

Your device asks our proxy for the weather.

Our proxy has to receive the connection to answer it, so it briefly sees your IP, the same way any website does. It uses your coordinates to fetch the forecast, then discards the connection details. It writes no log tied to you: no IP, no coordinates, no device ID.

Hop 2 · Proxy → weather provider

The provider only ever sees the proxy.

We build a brand-new request to the weather provider containing only the location and the query. Your IP, device ID, and app headers are never forwarded. To the provider, every Quiet Sky user looks like one anonymous source.

What we never collect

  • No account, no sign-in, no email.
  • No advertising ID, and no third-party analytics or tracker SDKs in the app.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data, because we do not hold it.
  • No record of your weather requests tied to you.

What the proxy does keep

To run the service we count anonymous totals only: how many requests went to each provider, and aggregate performance numbers. These carry no IP, no coordinates, and no device, and cannot be traced back to a person. Our hosting platform may keep short-lived operational edge logs on its own infrastructure, which we do not import, retain, or use to profile you.

Location precision is your choice

By default, Quiet Sky requests the forecast at full precision so the minute-by-minute nowcast is accurate. If you prefer, you can lower the precision the app sends in Settings. Either way, the provider never receives your IP, device, or identity.

Watch it yourself

You do not have to take our word for any of this. The in-app Privacy Dashboard logs every request the app makes, in plain language: where it went, what was sent, and what came back. The privacy claim is a mechanism you can audit, not a promise on a page.

Terms, in brief

Quiet Sky is provided as-is for personal weather use. Forecasts come from third-party providers and may be inaccurate; do not rely on them for safety-critical decisions. Subscriptions are billed through Google Play and follow Google Play's refund and cancellation terms. We may update these terms and this policy; material changes will be noted here with a new date above.
