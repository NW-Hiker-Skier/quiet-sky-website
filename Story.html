Why I built a private weather app that can't track you.

The forecast is yours.

A weather app should tell you about the sky, not turn your life into a market.

The sky doesn't ask anything of you.

It doesn't want your name. It doesn't follow you home. It doesn't sell where you slept last night to people you'll never meet. It just is. Open, overhead, the same for everyone.

Somewhere along the way, the apps that tell us about the sky forgot this.

They learned that the weather was never the product. You were. Every time you checked the rain, you paid. Not in money, but in yourself. Where you go, when you go, who you are with, fed to thousands of advertisers and brokers buying and selling your days in an auction you never knew was running.

You are a person, not a pattern of coordinates. Not a profile assembled behind your back. Not a line item in a market you never agreed to join.

We believe privacy is a right. The right to move through your life without being watched, catalogued, and sold without your full and knowing consent. A weather app should never be the thing that takes that from you.

So we built one that gives it back. Quiet Sky frees you from the watching. It tells you about the sky and asks for nothing in return but the price on the label. You are the customer here, never the inventory. The app works for you, and only you.

We refuse to make beauty the price of trust. The sky you check should be calm, clear, honest. Privacy you can see, and never have to think about.

The sky was never supposed to come with surveillance.

We're giving it back.

Quiet Sky. The forecast is yours. You are nobody's product.
